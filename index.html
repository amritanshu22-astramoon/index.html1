Dear Mom,

Happy Birthday to the most important woman in my life.

I know I don't always say it as often as I should, but I notice the little things you do.

The care, the patience, the countless small sacrifices, and the way you somehow manage to make ordinary days feel special.

When I look back at my life, so many of my favourite memories have you somewhere in them.

And when I think about the future, one of the biggest things I want is to make you proud and give you reasons to smile.

Thank you for believing in me even when I doubt myself.

Thank you for being there through every difficult day and every happy one.

Whatever I become, a part of it will always belong to you.

Today, I just want you to know that you are loved more than these few words can ever explain.

May this year bring you good health, peaceful days, beautiful surprises, and all the happiness you deserve.

Happy Birthday, Mom. ❤️
— Your Son
